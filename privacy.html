YouTubeAvto - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

YouTubeAvto is a personal tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With your permission, the tool accesses your YouTube channel's basic information (channel name) and permission to upload videos to that channel. It reads video files only from folders on the owner's own computer.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload videos and read the channel name. No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How to revoke access and delete data

You can revoke access at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the tool's folder on the computer removes all stored tokens.
